TrailTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

TrailTracker is provided by Otto Sponring.

Location

When you start a trip and grant precise location permission, the app uses your device's GPS location to calculate your route, distance, direction, altitude, and trip time. Tracking can continue while the screen is off, and the app displays a tracking notification.

Storage and sharing

The app keeps the current trip and trail in device memory. They remain available after you stop tracking but are lost when you start a new trip or the app process ends, including when the device restarts. The app does not upload your trail or location to the developer, and it does not include advertising or analytics services. Android and the device's location provider may process location information under their own terms and settings.

Your choices

You can pause or stop tracking in the app, and you can revoke its location permission in Android settings. Starting a new trip clears the previous trail.

Contact
